<h1 align="center">Tableau Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard (2023)</h1>

<p align="center">
  <strong>Interactive Business Intelligence Solution built with Tableau</strong><br/>
  <em>Sales Performance • Profitability Analysis • Customer Insights</em>
</p>

<hr/>

<h2>📌 Executive Summary</h2>
<p>
This project delivers a <strong>Tableau-based Business Intelligence solution</strong> designed to analyze
<strong>sales performance, profitability, and customer behavior</strong> for the year 2023.
The solution includes <strong>two interactive dashboards</strong> supported by a
<strong>centralized global filter pane</strong>, enabling self-service and data-driven analysis
across product and geographic dimensions.
</p>

<hr/>

<h2>🧩 Solution Architecture</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Sales Dashboard</strong> – Revenue, profit, quantity, and trend analysis</li>
  <li><strong>Customer Dashboard</strong> – Customer growth, order behavior, and profitability insights</li>
  <li><strong>Global Filter Pane</strong> – Dynamic filtering by product and location attributes</li>
</ul>

<hr/>

<h2>📊 Dashboards Overview</h2>

<h3>🔹 Sales Dashboard</h3>
<p><strong>Purpose:</strong> Monitor business performance and identify growth and risk areas.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Executive KPIs: <strong>Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Quantity</strong></li>
  <li><strong>Year-over-Year (YoY)</strong> performance comparison</li>
  <li>Monthly trend analysis with <strong>highest and lowest month indicators</strong></li>
  <li><strong>Sales & Profit by Product Sub-Category</strong>, including loss-making segments</li>
  <li>Weekly Sales and Profit trends with <strong>above/below average benchmarks</strong></li>
</ul>

<hr/>

<h3>🔹 Customer Dashboard</h3>
<p><strong>Purpose:</strong> Analyze customer behavior and revenue contribution.</p>

<ul>
  <li>KPIs: <strong>Total Customers, Total Orders, Sales per Customer</strong></li>
  <li>Customer growth trends over time</li>
  <li><strong>Customer distribution by number of orders</strong></li>
  <li>Top customers ranked by <strong>profitability, sales volume, and order frequency</strong></li>
</ul>

<hr/>

<h2>🎛️ Interactivity & Filters</h2>
<p>
A centralized filter panel enables real-time analysis by:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Year</li>
  <li>Product Category</li>
  <li>Product Sub-Category</li>
  <li>Region</li>
  <li>State</li>
  <li>City</li>
</ul>

<p>
This design supports <strong>drill-down and comparative analysis</strong> without cluttering the dashboards.
</p>
